## Purpose
Use this as an environmental storytelling trace that implies a story without showing the actor or explaining the event.

## Emotional Target
A small piece of human evidence inside an inhuman or empty place.

## Design Rules
- Place this only where the object physically and socially makes sense, unless intentionally using category contamination.
- Preserve absence: do not immediately reveal the person or cause behind it.
- The trace should create the listed question in the player’s mind.
- Use realistic scale, material, wear, lighting, and placement.
- If the object becomes decorative clutter, remove it.
