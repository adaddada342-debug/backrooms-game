# Make sound a structural material

## Purpose
Atomic core principle from the Sensory category.

## Source Extract
**Make sound a structural material.** Why: hum, reverb, vent noise, drips, distant machinery all imply scale and occupancy. Example: fluorescent buzz louder than expected. In game: treat ambience as navigation and dread, not as wallpaper.
