# Let the player hear what they cannot verify

## Purpose
Atomic core principle from the Sensory category.

## Source Extract
**Let the player hear what they cannot verify.** Why: unconfirmed stimuli preserve ambiguity. Example: a trolley wheel, distant phone ring, water slap. In game: sounds that never resolve into enemy encounters.
