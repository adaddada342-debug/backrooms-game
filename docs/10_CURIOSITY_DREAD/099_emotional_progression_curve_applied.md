# Emotional Progression Curve Applied

## Core Curve

1. Strange calm.
2. Investigative curiosity.
3. False familiarity.
4. Pattern formation.
5. Pattern betrayal.
6. Solitary endurance.
7. Trace attachment.
8. Archival obsession.
9. Existential scale shock.
10. Derealised acclimatisation.

## Implementation

### First Hour
- Strong sensory identity.
- Low explicit danger.
- Clear player control.
- Weirdness mostly environmental.

### First Ten Hours
- More level types.
- More traces of unseen humans.
- Partial mapping and route memory.
- Early contradictions.

### First Hundred Hours
- Procedural macro-structure becomes less predictable.
- Player competence increases, but full mastery remains impossible.
- The player starts building rituals: checking lights, naming halls, marking anchors, archiving traces.

### Late Play
- The Backrooms becomes routine.
- Normal reality should feel less reliable by contrast.
- Horror becomes adaptation, not attack.
