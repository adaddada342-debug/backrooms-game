## Codex Rule
Before creating a level, Codex must identify:

1. the level's emotional thesis
2. the familiar real-world typology
3. the dominant contradiction
4. the spatial grammar
5. the repetition/drift pattern
6. the sound identity
7. the material identity
8. the evidence-of-absence layer
9. the procedural generator requirements
10. the failure modes to avoid
