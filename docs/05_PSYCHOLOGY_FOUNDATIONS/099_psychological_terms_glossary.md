# Psychological Terms Glossary

## Affective Design
Designing primarily for emotional/cognitive state rather than for explicit plot, combat, or collectible progression.

## Ambiguous Threat
A condition where the player cannot determine whether danger exists, where it comes from, or whether the environment itself is the problem.

## Derealisation-Like Unreality
A design target where ordinary reality begins to feel unstable, dreamlike, or less trustworthy. This is a fictional design concept, not a clinical simulation.

## Liminality
A threshold state: between places, roles, times, or meanings. In level design this means spaces built for passing through, waiting, transition, and non-arrival.

## Nostalgic Incompletion
A memory cue without the people, context, or social event that would normally complete it.

## Scale Without Informational Closure
A space feels enormous not merely because it is large, but because the player cannot verify its bounds or meaning.

## Trace Attachment
A stage where ordinary objects become emotionally important because they imply absent people or personal histories.
