# Evidence of People Over People

## Purpose
The report identifies evidence of people as scarier than people themselves because it preserves absence while proving presence.

## Emotional Target
presence implied through absence

## Source Basis
Derived from the uploaded Backrooms research report and its environmental storytelling library.

## Design Rules
- Use recent-use details without showing users.
- Let the player infer that someone was here recently.
- Do not immediately resolve traces into NPC encounters.

## Implementation Notes
- Examples: warm chair, damp towel, blinking voicemail, recently replaced bulb.
- Use freshness gradients: old dust, recent handprint, active machine.

## Codex Instructions
- Choose traces that make the player ask where the person went.
- Avoid turning every trace into a quest marker.

## Common Failure Modes
- Using props as filler.
- Explaining too much.
- Creating quest clutter.
- Making traces too dramatic too often.
