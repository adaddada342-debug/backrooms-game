## Source Thesis Alignment
The Backrooms project is an affective design problem. It must preserve liminality, absence, scale, architectural wrongness, nostalgia, solitude, curiosity, and derealised exploration. Do not drift into generic monster horror, combat loops, SCP-style explanation dumps, or streamer-bait chase rooms.
