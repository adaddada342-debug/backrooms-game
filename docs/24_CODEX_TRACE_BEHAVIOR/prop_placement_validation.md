# Prop Placement Validation

## Purpose
Codex must validate every prop as evidence, not decoration.

## Emotional Target
disciplined environmental implication

## Source Basis
Derived from the uploaded Backrooms research report and its environmental storytelling library.

## Design Rules
- Ask what the prop proves.
- Ask what the prop hides.
- Ask what question it creates.
- Ask why it belongs in that exact location.

## Implementation Notes
- Before adding a prop list, classify each as Trace, Utility, Landmark, Noise, or PlayerTool.
- Delete Noise unless it supports realism at very low density.

## Codex Instructions
- Follow this before adding authored or procedural environmental details.
- Prefer fewer, stronger traces.
- Preserve the project thesis: absence, scale, wrongness, curiosity.

## Common Failure Modes
- Monster explanations.
- Decoration spam.
- Lore dump notes.
- Quest clutter.
- Jumpscare setup disguised as environmental storytelling.
