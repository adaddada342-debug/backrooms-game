There is still no single formal discipline called “Backrooms studies”, so several conclusions here are necessarily synthetic rather than direct laboratory findings about the phenomenon itself. Some useful academic sources were accessible only through abstracts or snippets, and community evidence is heterogeneous because the Backrooms has multiple canons and anti-canon communities with conflicting preferences.
